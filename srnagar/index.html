Keratin Smoothing

From Frizzy to Glass-Smooth

Deep molecular keratin protein infusion that transforms unmanageable, frizzy, curly hair into sleek, straight, reflective hair. Results last 4-6 months with proper care.

Duration 2.5 – 3 Hours
Price ₹3,500 (Onwards)
Lasts 4-6 Months

Treatment Process

  1. Hair health analysis & clarifying pH-balance wash
  2. Section-by-section keratin protein application
  3. Flat iron sealing at 230°C to lock proteins into cuticle
  4. Cool rinse, conditioning mask & finishing blowout
